Scout Club

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This policy explains what data Scout Club ("the app") collects, why, and how it's used. The app is a football club management game published by Kadir Kıran.

DATA WE COLLECT

When you play Scout Club, we collect:

DataPurpose
Anonymous account identifierLets your club, squad, and progress persist between sessions, without requiring a name, email, or any personal account.
Gameplay data (club name, squad, match results, in-game currency balances, settings)Core game functionality — this is what makes your save work.
Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, or equivalent)Used by Google AdMob to show and measure rewarded and interstitial ads. You can limit this via your device's ad tracking settings.
Basic device/diagnostic informationUsed by Firebase and Google AdMob for crash reporting and service reliability.

We do not require or collect your real name, email address, phone number, or any government-issued ID to play.

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Scout Club uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and cloud data storage for your club/save data.
  • Google AdMob — serves the rewarded and interstitial ads in the app.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Scout Club does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the anonymous, non-identifying data described above. The app does not require a real name, email, or other personal identifiers to play.

DATA DELETION

To request deletion of your club/save data, email us at kadirkran1@gmail.com with your club name and approximate start date. We'll remove your data from our systems.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change too. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy? Email kadirkran1@gmail.com.
