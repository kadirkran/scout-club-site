COMMON QUESTIONS

I lost my progress / my club disappeared.
Your club is tied to the anonymous sign-in on this device. If you deleted the app or changed devices, the sign-in may be new. Email us with your Manager ID (if you saved it), your club name and the approximate date you started playing.

How do I delete my data?
Email us from an address you can prove is yours with your Manager ID. We will delete your club data and confirm. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I report an offensive club name?
Open the manager leaderboard in the game and tap the flag next to the club, or email us.

RESPONSE TIME

We aim to reply within a few business days.
